SF Music List

product · music · web

San Francisco has always had one of the richest live music scenes in the world — but finding out what's actually happening tonight, at which venue, with which band, has always been harder than it should be. SF Music List solves that. It's a daily-updated guide to live music in San Francisco: every show, every venue, surfaced and organized so you can spend less time searching and more time listening.

What It Does

The site pulls in current show listings across SF's venues — from intimate clubs to larger stages — and updates them daily so the information is always fresh. Each listing surfaces the essentials: the artist, the venue, the date. From there, you can dig deeper: find the venue on a map, listen to the artist's top tracks, or tune into the built-in radio feature to discover music you've never heard before.

The radio feature is one of the things I'm most proud of. Instead of just listing names, it gives you an instant on-ramp to the music itself. You hear something you like, you look up where they're playing, you go. That's the loop SF Music List is designed to close.

The Problem It Solves

Most event discovery tools are built around aggregation at scale — they want to surface everything everywhere. SF Music List has a narrower, more deliberate focus: one city, one scene, updated every day. That specificity is the point. San Francisco has an embarrassment of great venues — the Fillmore, Great American Music Hall, Bottom of the Hill, the Independent, Slim's — and a constant rotation of artists passing through or calling the city home. The site makes that richness navigable.

It's designed for the kind of person who wants to find new music near them — not just browse a global calendar, but actually go somewhere tonight and hear something great.

Design & Build

SF Music List is a personal project I designed and built myself — the same instinct that drives my product work applied to something I care about outside of it. Clean, fast, minimal. It loads quickly, works on mobile, and gets out of the way so the music can be the focus. The daily update cadence keeps it useful without requiring constant manual curation.
