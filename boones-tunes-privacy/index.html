Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Boone's Tunes ("the app," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with your use of the app. Boone's Tunes is an independent application developed by Boone Spooner. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

By downloading, installing, or using Boone's Tunes, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Information We Collect

Boone's Tunes is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. Depending on how you use the app, the following categories of information may be involved:

  • Information you provide directly. If you contact us for support or feedback, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your email address and the contents of your message.
  • App preferences and settings. Your in-app settings, playback preferences, and library organization are stored locally on your device to make the app work as you expect.
  • Device and usage information. We may collect limited, non-identifying technical information — such as device type, operating system version, app version, and crash diagnostics — to keep the app stable and to diagnose problems.

Boone's Tunes does not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect names, precise location, contacts, photos, or other sensitive personal information.

Music and Media Access

Where the app plays or organizes music, it accesses media and audio files on your device solely to provide playback and library features. This access is used locally on your device. We do not upload, copy, or transmit your personal media files to our servers or to any third party.

How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above only to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the app's features;
  • Remember your preferences and settings;
  • Diagnose crashes and technical issues and improve stability and performance;
  • Respond to your support requests and communications.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your information for advertising profiles or targeted advertising.

Data Storage and Retention

Your settings and preferences are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Any diagnostic information is retained only for as long as needed to identify and resolve issues, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Third-Party Services

Boone's Tunes may rely on trusted third-party platform services — such as Google Play services and standard crash-reporting or analytics tools provided by the operating system — to deliver and maintain the app. These services process only the limited technical data necessary for their function and are governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy for information about how Google handles data on the Play platform.

Children's Privacy

Boone's Tunes is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Your Choices and Rights

You can control the app's access to media, storage, and other permissions at any time through your device's system settings. You may also delete all locally stored app data by uninstalling the app. If you would like to ask a question about your information or request its deletion, contact us using the details below.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be reflected here, and your continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
