Boone's Tunes

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Boone's Tunes ("the app," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with your use of the app. Boone's Tunes is an independent alarm clock application developed by Boone Spooner. We are committed to protecting your privacy, and the app is built to respect it by design.

By downloading, installing, or using Boone's Tunes, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

The Short Version

Boone's Tunes is an alarm app. It does not require an account or login, it does not connect to any external service or API, and it does not collect, store, or share any information about you or how you use the app. The only data we ever receive is anonymous diagnostic information used to fix problems and improve the app.

Information We Do Not Collect

Boone's Tunes does not:

  • Require you to create an account or sign in;
  • Ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier;
  • Track, log, or store how you use the app, including your alarms, schedules, or settings on our side;
  • Access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or other sensitive device data;
  • Connect to any external server or third-party API to send or receive your information;
  • Sell, rent, or share any personal information, because we do not collect any.

Your Alarms and Settings Stay on Your Device

The alarms, times, tones, and preferences you set up are stored locally on your device so the app can function. This information never leaves your device — it is not uploaded to us or to anyone else — and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Diagnostic Information

The only information the app may transmit is anonymous diagnostic data — such as crash reports, error logs, and basic technical details like device model, operating system version, and app version. This information is used solely to identify and fix bugs, improve stability, and make the app better over time. It does not identify you personally and is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

On Android, this diagnostic reporting is provided through the standard Google Play platform services. You can review and manage how crash and diagnostic data is shared through your device's system settings and your Google account privacy controls.

Children's Privacy

Boone's Tunes is not directed to children under the age of 13, and because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Choices

You can control the app's permissions at any time through your device's system settings, and you can remove all locally stored app data by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct, or delete on our side.

Security

Because Boone's Tunes keeps your alarms and settings on your own device and does not transmit personal information, your data stays under your control. We take reasonable care with the limited, anonymous diagnostic information the app produces.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
